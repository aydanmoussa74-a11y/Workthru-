# Product Overview — Workout PWA

## Core Identity
Workout PWA is a mobile-first, local-first, interactive home-training companion.

**Core Promise:**
> Open → Follow → Train → Progress.

## Product Principles
1. **Follow, Don't Figure It Out:** Immediate access to guided workouts without decision friction.
2. **Demonstrate, Don't Just Explain:** Visual movement demonstrations prioritized over dense text.
3. **Progress, Don't Punish:** Capability and progression over shaming or unsustainable volume.
4. **Capability Over Appearance:** Focused on movement proficiency, strength, mobility, and habit consistency.
5. **AI Assists; System Governs:** AI operates strictly within bounded application tool contracts.
6. **Local First:** Offline-capable core workout player and local state persistence.
7. **Simplicity Before Decoration:** High contrast, legible typography, and athletic focus.
